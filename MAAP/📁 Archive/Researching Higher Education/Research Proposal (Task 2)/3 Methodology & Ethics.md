### **3.1 Research Design**

- **Approach:** Qualitative / exploratory (reason: to understand student experiences and ideas in depth)
    
- **Rationale:** Why qualitative over quantitative? How does it align with your research question?
    
- **Type of Study:** Case study in a single module / cohort
    

---

### **3.2 Participants**

- **Population:** First-year engineering students from non-computing backgrounds
    
- **Sample Size:** Approx. ____ students (decide based on feasibility)
    
- **Sampling Method:** Purposive / convenience sampling — why this method?
    
- **Recruitment:** Email invitation via module leader / in-class announcement
    

---

### **3.3 Data Collection**

- **Methods:**
    
    - Option A: Online survey (mix of Likert-scale + open-ended questions)
        
    - Option B: Focus groups (small groups of 4–6)
        
    - Option C: Combination of both for richer data
        
- **Timing:** Week ____ of semester to capture authentic experiences
    
- **Location:** On-campus / online via Teams / hybrid
    
- **Materials:** Survey tool (e.g., Qualtrics, Microsoft Forms), discussion guide
    

---

### **3.4 Data Analysis**

- **Approach:** Thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006)
    
- **Process:** Familiarisation → coding → theme generation → theme review → write-up
    
- **Tools:** Manual coding / software like NVivo or Excel
    
- **Reliability Checks:** Second coder for a subset of data / peer review of themes
    

---

### **3.5 Ethical Considerations**

- **Ethical Approval:** Required via City, University of London ethics process
    
- **Key Issues:**
    
    - Informed consent before participation
        
    - Anonymity and confidentiality maintained
        
    - Right to withdraw at any stage without penalty
        
    - Data stored securely on university systems
        
    - Awareness of insider research if you have a teaching role with participants
        
- **Mitigation:** Clear participant information sheet and consent form
