
# ✅ **1. SIMPLE FORMAT (One-sentence criteria)**

This is the most common style used in assignment briefs.

### **Example Criteria (for an essay assessment):**

1. **Demonstrates a clear understanding of the topic and addresses all parts of the question.**
    
2. **Critically analyses relevant literature and integrates evidence to support arguments.**
    
3. **Communicates ideas clearly with appropriate academic structure, style, and referencing.**
    
4. **Applies theoretical frameworks appropriately to the chosen case study.**
    

Clean, focused, and one line each.

---

# ✅ **2. EXPANDED FORMAT (With performance indicators)**

Used when you want clearer expectations for students.  
This is **great for your 1000-word assignment brief**.

### **Example:**

**Criterion 1: Understanding and Application of Theory (40%)**

- Demonstrates accurate understanding of key concepts.
    
- Applies theory appropriately to the assessment task.
    
- Uses relevant evidence to justify decisions.
    

**Criterion 2: Quality of Analysis (30%)**

- Shows depth, critique, and evaluation.
    
- Considers multiple perspectives.
    
- Provides a logical and evidence-based argument.
    

**Criterion 3: Communication and Structure (20%)**

- Writes clearly and cohesively.
    
- Uses appropriate academic style.
    
- Follows required format and word count.
    

**Criterion 4: Referencing (10%)**

- Uses accurate in-text citations.
    
- Provides a complete reference list.

# ✅ **3. RUBRIC-STYLE CRITERIA (Highly structured)**

If your assignment brief uses grade bands (e.g., 70+, 60–69), this is how it looks.

### **Example:**

|**Criterion**|**Pass (50–59%)**|**Merit (60–69%)**|**Distinction (70%+)**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Critical Analysis**|Describes ideas with limited critique|Analyses key issues with some critical depth|Strong, sustained critique with synthesis|
|**Use of Literature**|Uses some sources|Integrates relevant literature|Uses high-quality sources critically and effectively|
|**Structure & Clarity**|Generally clear|Well organised|Highly coherent and professionally presented|

This is too long for your brief unless you choose a short version.

---

# ✅ **4. SMART CRITERIA (as used in your module videos)**

These link each criterion **directly to a Learning Outcome**.

### **Example:**

**Criterion: Evaluate and synthesise relevant literature (Aligned to LO2)**

- Specific
    
- Measurable
    
- Appropriate
    
- Realistic
    
- Testable
    

You would write it as:

> **“You will be assessed on your ability to evaluate and synthesise relevant literature to support your arguments (LO2).”**
