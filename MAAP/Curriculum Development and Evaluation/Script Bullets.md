### 🔹 **Intro**

- Aim: redesign labs to build practical, industry-relevant skills
    
- Will cover: challenges → design objectives → learning approaches → tools → assessment → evaluation
    

---

### 🔹 **Why Redesign?**

- EE2703 = core second-year biomedical instrumentation module
    
- Covers: monitoring, circuits, ECG, patient data
    
- Problem: theory–practice gap, passive learning
    
- Solution: flipped, project-based, inclusive design using real-world tools
    

---

### 🔹 **Frameworks & Objectives**

- TEF: focus on active learning, outcomes, employability
    
- City Inclusive Framework: scaffolded support, cross-module synergy
    
- QAA: embed technical skills like coding
    
- BERA: ethical evaluation, informed consent, anonymity
    

---

### 🔹 **Stakeholders & Skills**

- Feedback from NHS, industry, alumni, faculty
    
- Needs: coding (Python), systems thinking, signal analysis
    
- Result: hands-on, real-world biomedical systems
    
- Transferable skills: tech, data, consulting, research
    

---

### 🔹 **Flipped Learning**

- Students prep at home (videos, quizzes, notebooks)
    
- Lab time = applied work, collaboration, feedback
    
- Improves engagement, understanding, confidence
    
- Strong evidence base in engineering education
    

---

### 🔹 **Project-Based Learning**

- Students solve real biomedical problems in teams
    
- Example: ECG → Python → app/dashboard
    
- Develops creativity, communication, critical thinking
    
- Prepares for MedTech, R&D, digital health
    

---

### 🔹 **Inclusive Learning**

- Scaffolded bootcamp + guided tasks for beginners
    
- Open-ended projects + extension tasks for advanced
    
- Supports all learners to grow in confidence and competence
    
- Inclusive by design — not afterthought
    

---

### 🔹 **Tools & Delivery**

- 🎯 **Skill**: Independent preparation → 🧰 **Support**: Videos, virtual simulations
    
- 🎯 **Skill**: Group work & communication → 🧰 **Support**: Live coding, GitHub, Jupyter
    
- Tools matched to learning goals — not just content delivery
    

---

### 🔹 **Assessment & Feedback**

- 🔄 Formative: quizzes, in-lab feedback, peer review
    
- 📄 Project Portfolio: reflection, documentation
    
- 💻 Prototype: test practical skills
    
- 🗣 Viva: justify decisions, problem-solve live
    
- Balanced mix: assess & develop skills
    

---

### 🔹 **Evaluation & Continuous Improvement**

- Collect: surveys, focus groups, performance data
    
- Analyse: identify trends and areas to improve
    
- Refine: adjust tools, pacing, scaffolding
    
- Share: internal + external dissemination
    
- BERA-compliant, student-informed, ongoing process
    
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Students as co-creators: their input shapes future labs
    

---

### 🔹 **Summary & Close**

- Not just content redesign — full **pedagogical shift**
    
- Flipped + interactive delivery = better prep
    
- Projects = deeper learning + collaboration
    
- Tools + assessment = transferable, employable skills
    
- Prepares students for real-world biomedical and tech careers
